# Credits

Rollin embeds the sounds below. Every one is public domain under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required by the
licence and is given here anyway. `tools/pack_assets.py` copies them from the downloaded kits into `Assets/sounds/`.

The tuning parts are built from Schedule I's own meshes and materials at runtime; Rollin ships no models.

## Sounds (`Assets/sounds/`)

| File | Source asset | Author | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bicycle.pcm` | `bicycle-horn-1.wav` | AntumDeluge | https://opengameart.org/content/bicycle-horn | CC0 |

The sounds are stored as 16-bit mono PCM at 22050 Hz. The clown, duck, train, "Charge!" and La Cucaracha horns are
synthesised by the mod at load time; La Cucaracha is a traditional tune in the public domain.
