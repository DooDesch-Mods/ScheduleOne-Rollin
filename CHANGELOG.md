# Changelog

All notable changes to Rollin are documented in this file.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/), and this
project adheres to Semantic Versioning.

## [1.0.4] - 2026-10-21

### Changed
- The tailpipes smoke like a car exhaust while you sit in the chair.
- The tyres squeal like a car's when the chair slides.

### Fixed
- The jerry can rocket made a thruster noise, and the tailpipes popped while you drove.
- The tailpipes pointed the wrong way round.
- WASD moved the chair while the tuning menu was open.
- "Open Moving Box" showed above the seat instead of at the box.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- The Moving Box has 4 slots: press E at the box while the chair stands still. What you put in is saved.
- Trim paint also colours the chair's grips, armrests and footrests.

### Changed
- A tuned chair stays parked where you get out, and it is still there after a reload.
- In the tuning menu the camera follows the mouse, no right-click needed.

### Fixed
- The chair lost its tuning when you got out.
- You sat too far back, and white boxes showed around the chair while sitting.
- The exhaust looks like in 1.0.1 again instead of two plain pipes.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-21

### Fixed
- You sit on the cushion now, instead of hovering above it and leaning over your knees.
- Getting out no longer throws the chair across the map.
- The chair keeps its own colour when you sit down.
- Spoiler, roof and light pole, exhaust, muscle rims and hat sit where they belong. Trim paint colours the parts.
- Less lag while you sit in a chair.

### Changed
- The tuning menu opens with R, because T also opened the phone. Change the key with `GarageKey`.
- The jerry can booster has no flame.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-21

### Changed
- New icon and header: the stock wheelchair next to the same chair with full tuning. The mod itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- Sit in the wheelchairs around Hyland Point and roll them with W, A, S and D. A second player can take the handles and
  push.
- Press T in a wheelchair for the Rollin Garage: paint, trim paint, neon, six horns and stat upgrades, free of charge.
- Strap a jerry can rocket to the backrest, bolt on car rims, twin tailpipes that backfire, a police lightbar and an
  armrest radio.
- Park a garden gnome between your legs or wear its hat. Neon glows from under the seat.
- In co-op the rider, the pusher and every part sync, and the host saves each chair's tuning.
